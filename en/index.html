FAQ

Questions people usually ask

How long does it take?

1–2 weeks, depending on how quickly I get your photos and details. Google's verification of the profile can take a few more days.

What if I need changes later?

With monthly care, just tell me and I'll change it. Without it, I still do it for a price agreed in advance.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The domain and hosting are in your name, you own the business profile, and you get every file. If you ever move elsewhere, you don't need anything from me.

Do you only work locally?

No, I work across Hungary. Phone, video call and email are enough for everything; we never have to meet in person.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. Tell me what you do and send a few photos from your phone. I handle the rest.

Can you guarantee I'll be first on Google?

Nobody can honestly promise that. What I guarantee is a complete, accurate profile and website, because that is the basis for showing up in local searches.

What do I need to prepare?

Your business details (name, address, tax number), your services, opening hours and 5–10 photos of your work or premises. No good photos? I'll help.
